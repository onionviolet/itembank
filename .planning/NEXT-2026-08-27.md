## What is genuinely unblocked, and can proceed without Weibao

Ordered by value, honestly assessed.

### 1. Phase 14C planning, the largest unblocked block

14C depends on **14A only**, which is frozen. It has a context document
(`14C-CONTEXT.md`) and a stated shape of eight plans, one wave each, and no
plans written yet. It is the source adapter registry: one adapter contract and

It also **blocks the source-binding half of 14B**, which makes it the one piece
of unblocked work that shortens the critical path rather than running beside it.
This is the recommended next agent task.

### 2. The workspace object, IL-20260826-01

The Navigate2 teardown narrowed to exactly one missing durable object: the

It has no phase and no requirement. It is small, it is the top line of the
shelf mockup, and it is the only structural gap between what ships and what the
mockup draws. Worth a requirement and a home before 16B-04 is executed, because
16B-04 will otherwise invent one.
