### 1. Phase 14C, and a correction to this document

**Corrected 2026-08-27, same day, before this file was acted on.** The first
draft of this section said 14C had "no plans written yet" and recommended
planning it as the largest unblocked block. That was wrong, and checking took
one `ls`. **All eight plans exist**, `14C-01-PLAN.md` through `14C-08-PLAN.md`,
alongside `14C-CONTEXT.md`, `14C-PATTERNS.md`, `14C-RESEARCH.md`,
`14C-VALIDATION.md` and `COVERAGE.md`. 14C is planned and ready to execute, not
to plan.

**But it is not unblocked either**, which is the more important half of the
correction:

- `14C-01` carries a **blocking decision checkpoint**, rated a one-way door:
  the exact field names of `schemas/source_locator.schema.json`, and whether a
  source with an adapter and a locator sidecar becomes the primary source
  representation or is added alongside the Markdown-only path.
- `14C-07` carries a second: whether stdlib `zipfile` plus `xml.etree` is the
  accepted EPUB path, with `ebooklib` parked alongside PyMuPDF pending an
  explicit **AGPL** decision.
- The phase's dependency choices (pdfplumber pinned with pdfminer.six,
  python-docx) run through `SUPPLY-CHAIN-POLICY.md` section 3, and there is no
  `VENDORED.md` at the repository root, so **nothing is adopted yet**. That is
  the same gate the 17A-04 Playwright packet is waiting on.

So 14C does not shorten the critical path by being started today. It still
blocks the source-binding half of 14B and remains the highest-value phase to
unblock, but unblocking it means two more decisions and a supply-chain
approval, not agent work.

**Revised recommendation:** fold 14C's two checkpoints and its dependency
approval into the same sitting as the 14B packet, rather than treating 14C as
parallel work an agent can just begin.
