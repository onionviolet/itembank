<objective>
Make "the last N responses in this bank" cheaply answerable. The disposable sqlite3 index
has no `bank` column today and `objective_history()`'s rows carry neither `bank` nor
`objective`, so the bank-scoped cooldown SEL-03 needs cannot be asked for through the fast
path at all. Bump `INDEX_VERSION`, add the column and the index, widen the projected row,
and expose the filter on `itembank evidence --bank`.

Purpose: D-13 chose bank-scoped cooldown over subject-scoped precisely because the index is
disposable and rebuildable, which makes a schema bump the cheap answer rather than the
expensive one. This plan pays that cost once, in the module that owns it, rather than
letting a Phase 7 selector reach around `evidence.py` and grow a second history reader.

Output: `INDEX_VERSION = 2`, a `bank` column with its own composite index, `objective` and
`bank` on every returned row, a `bank=` parameter threaded through the predicate and both
halves of the query, and a CLI flag that proves the capability has a surface.
</objective>
