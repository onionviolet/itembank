> **Phase goal (quoted verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` § Phase 7 — this line is
> NOT in user-story form; run `/gsd mvp-phase 7` to convert it before the next replan):**
> A session is assembled by rule, not by hand — targeted at an objective, a difficulty,
> or a discrimination pair — and the tool can say why it picked each item.

<objective>
Give the four selection modes their compositions and give the selector its memory. One
table maps each mode name to a filter set, an ordering and a count policy (D-06); one
hard/soft exposure split reads the bank-scoped evidence history plan 07-03 made
answerable (D-08). After this plan a learner gets a diagnostic that spreads, a
remediation that concentrates on what they got wrong, an exam that ignores their history
entirely, and no accidental repeats of what they just answered.

Purpose: SEL-02 and SEL-03 are the two requirements this phase exists for, and both are
easy to fake — four differently-named code paths that produce the same session, or a
cooldown that only remembers what is still in the session file. The mode table makes the
first checkable by inspecting one data structure; reading exposure from the evidence log
rather than the session JSON makes the second true across a resumed sitting.

Scope fence: this plan ships the soft-penalty *seam* and reads one existing weight.
Weighting models, decay curves, due dates, mastery drop-out and the daily cap are Phase 10
(TREND-01, SCHED-01/02) and are not built here.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-02 | Denial of Service | history read on every selection request | medium | mitigate | The caller reads the history exactly once per request, bank-scoped, through `objective_history()`'s indexed fast path (`idx_bank`, added by 07-03), with the linear-scan fallback as the degrade path. `select()` performs no read of its own and no per-item query; `cooldown_responses` is bounded 0..500 by the settings schema so the window can never be unbounded |
