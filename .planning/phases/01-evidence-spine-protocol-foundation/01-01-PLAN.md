<objective>
Answer the one question ROADMAP.md flags as blocking every other evidence-writing task in this
phase — **does a single-`write()` append actually survive on Windows?** — by building the log
primitive and measuring it on the target machine, before anything writes a real event.

Purpose: Python's `O_APPEND` on Windows is implemented by the MS C runtime as `lseek` +
`write`, two steps rather than one atomic step (bpo-42606, still open). D-07 makes the entire
evidence store depend on that append. RESEARCH.md says the spike belongs first and blocking, and
names exactly what it must measure. This plan is that spike plus the primitive it measures.

Output: `evidence.py` with the locked-append and defensive-read primitives,
`tests/durability_roundtrip.py` running the three measurements as part of the ordinary
`python tests/*.py` suite, `.gitignore` excluding `_evidence/`, and `01-SPIKE-RESULT.md`
recording the numbers.

<assumption_delta_decision>

(`_attempts/*.md`, `_attempts/session_*.json`, `daily_log.md`), each its own primary record with
its own writer. After it there is one append-only log of events, and each of the three is a

evidence store often ships as the only store *new* writes go to, while history sits unmigrated."
