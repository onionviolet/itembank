# Plan 999.4-03 Summary — AGS passback, hosting, and hardening

## What landed

The phase's third wave closes the loop: the runtime's score reaches the
Canvas gradebook exactly once at completion when the launch granted AGS --
and pending prose is never auto-graded (`PendingManual` or no publish). The
hosting posture is documented and testable both ways (stdlib TLS, reverse
proxy), the dependencies are pinned with checksums and a named license
review plus the CVE disposition, and the live-Canvas run is a documented
manual checklist rather than a guess.

A completed session with pending prose is unreachable through this build's
learner flow (short items defer without advancing until a marker settles
them, and marking does not yet advance the cursor). The PendingManual
branch is implemented and tested against the marker-closed state; a future
close-session feature makes it reachable end-to-end. Recorded in the
SUMMARY and VERIFICATION rather than hidden.
