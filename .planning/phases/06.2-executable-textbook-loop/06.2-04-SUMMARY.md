outside this phase's files).**

## Accomplishments

- The twelve UI-SPEC §13 gates are executable fixtures, each mapped in
  `06.2-GATES.md` to a named test and command: no-leak (all seven
  surfaces), zero-reflow (pre-band bytes across four states), the
  compatibility-floor pair (3.1's fixture bank, style-block exclusion
  named), focus (required reveal h2, recommended no-growth), degraded
  (12.1 copy, no reveal, no spinner), print (`?print=1` complete ungated
  with D1 `Check · <objective>` labels and an unchanged evidence log),
  skip (one event, no response, band live), mode-degrade (diagnostic +
  exam), responsive (all `[GATE:]` values × `gate_skip` values,
  `reader_nav: column`, viewport meta, wrap-only controls), calm-progress
  (no count/percentage/track/streak/glyph/exclamation/motion), voice
  (font-family token names, section-15 strings verbatim), and the
  cross-phase divergence guard (the shared `CHECK_UNRESOLVED_COPY`
  object, imported by identity).
- `tests/phase_062_audit.py` parses the four PLAN.md files' requirements
  fields and proves 6/6 GATE-01..06 IDs are covered by plans whose
  acceptance criteria name real test commands; it verifies the
  deterministic edge-probe skip sentence is recorded in every PLAN.md and
  that `itembank schema --all` emits cleanly.
- `fixtures/selection_evidence.jsonl` (pre-6.2 quiz-served records) gains
  the additive `context=quiz` field so the published response schema
  validates the checked-in history.
- `06.2-GATES.md` records the twelve gates, their fixtures, and the one
  human-verify item: true screen-reader announcement behavior on a fresh
  server-rendered page (the focus move is the primary channel and must
  carry the information on its own, section 7.2/14).
