# Phase 06.2 Plan 03: The Recorded-Skip Control, Check/Skip Routes, Settings, and the Outcome Split — Summary

**Reading ahead is a normal button, check and skip are real runtime actions
with honest evidence and honest degradation, and the outcome split is a
derived, stated-denominator table with nothing to game.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~80 min (Tasks 1-3 + closeout)
- **Completed:** 2026-08-10
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 11

## Accomplishments

- The recorded-skip control ships in the band: a real submit button labelled
  `Read ahead without answering` in the same `.actions` row as the check
  button (both ≥44px via the shared `.go` control), never styled as a
  transgression (C16). `gate_skip: after-attempt` states its condition in
  Ledger text (`Read ahead becomes available after one attempt.`) until one
  recorded attempt exists — never a disabled button (C9).
- `gate_skip` (`always` default) and `gate_policy` (`as-authored` default)
  register in the `reader` settings group with schema rows and checked-in
  defaults; `itembank config set` enforces validate-then-write and the
  weaken-only enum `[as-authored, off]` (section 10.1).
- `POST /lesson/<stem>/check` scores through the one shared
  `quiz.record_gate_check` path (runtime.score_response + response evidence
  with `context="lesson_gate"`) and issues a 303; under required with a
  cleared check the next section renders with `<h2 tabindex="-1" autofocus>`
  and the status region carries `The next section is below.` (7.1/7.2).
- `POST /lesson/<stem>/skip` records exactly one `gate_skip` event through
  the one shared `quiz.record_gate_skip` path (no response, no hint tier),
  reveals the next section, leaves the band live and answerable, and
  announces `Read ahead recorded. This check stays open.` A repeat skip
  dedupes to one event; a reload of the landing page never re-submits
  (post-redirect-get). Both routes sit behind the loopback authority gate.
- The degraded path: when the evidence log is unwritable the band renders
  `This check cannot be submitted while the runtime is unreachable. Run
  itembank daemon and reload.` with no reveal, no disabled-without-reason
  control, and no spinner (section 12.1, C9).
- The gate CLI twins `lesson-check` and `lesson-skip` reach the same
  runtime calls (SURF-04) and are registered in ROUTE_CLI.
- `gate_outcome_split(log, bank, session_id, gate_modes)` derives the
  cleared-vs-skipped split per distinct (session, check) pair — cleared
  wins over a prior skip, a skip with no response is skipped, the
  denominator counts distinct required pairs, and recommended pairs are
  excluded (a skip recorded as `recommended`, e.g. from a degraded sitting,
  excludes the pair). The `/report` page renders the denominator copy,
  the count/share table, and the empty state with no target, streak, or
  percentage-fill bar (D-10, section 11).

## Risks / Notes

- The `role=status` announcement reliability on a fresh page load is a
  recorded backstop (section 7.2/14): the focus move is the primary channel
  and carries the information on its own; the clause is additive. A real
  screen-reader check is the one human-verify item (recorded in plan-04's
  GATES.md).
- `EVENT_SCHEMA_VERSION` stays 2; the pre-6.2 response events without the
  `context` field are read by the version-tolerant `events()` reader, not
  by schema validation (flagged for Phase 10).
