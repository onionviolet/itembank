next idea appears, and the same idea returning on the normal retention

## The Twelve Verification Gates (UI-SPEC §13)

All twelve are executable fixtures mapped in `06.2-GATES.md`; each passes:

1. **No-leak** — below-gate content absent from HTML and text content;
   key/rationale/objective absent before the verdict
2. **Zero-reflow** — pre-band bytes byte-identical across inert/open/
   cleared/skipped; one `.gate` box
3. **Compatibility-floor pair** — 3.1's fixture bank byte-identical
4. **Focus** — required clear/skip targets the revealed `<h2 tabindex="-1"
   autofocus>`; recommended never grows the DOM; reload never re-submits
5. **Degraded** — 12.1 copy, no disabled-without-reason control, no
   spinner, no reveal
6. **Print** — `?print=1` complete ungated, D1 `Check · <objective>` rules,
   no boundary, evidence log unchanged
7. **Skip** — one `gate_skip`, no response, no hint-tier advance, band
   stays live
8. **Mode-degrade** — diagnostic/exam render complete with the exact line,
   no skip control, no `gate_skip`
9. **Responsive** — all `[GATE:]` × `gate_skip` combos, `reader_nav:
   column`, viewport meta, wrap-only controls
10. **Calm-progress** — no count/percentage/track/streak/glyph/
    exclamation/motion on band, reveal, or boundary
11. **Voice** — token-named font families (Ledger/Paper), section-15 copy
    verbatim
12. **Cross-phase divergence guard** — linter and renderer share the one
    `CHECK_UNRESOLVED_COPY` object
