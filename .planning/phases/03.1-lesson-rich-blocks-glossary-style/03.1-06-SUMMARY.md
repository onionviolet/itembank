   (4.005R, Regular + Semibold) and iA Writer Quattro (pinned commit) as
   woff2, each beside its OFL 1.1 license and RESERVED-NAMES note, with

   SHA-1, and sizes of all four woff2 against the manifest: **ALL_OK**.

   `presentation.py`; every surface consumes the tokens.

   the TERMS/glossary grammar, the [!KEY] card, the callout kinds, the
   Educational Objective line, and style files — the authoring contract for
   an agent with no repo context. `09-02-PLAN.md` now carries the
   `lesson_layout` enum in the subject_profiles entry contract (EMT/Math
   `separate`, CS `inline`) before Phase 9 executes, with no registry
   version bump (D-04).
