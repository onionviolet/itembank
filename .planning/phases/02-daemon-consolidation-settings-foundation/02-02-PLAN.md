<objective>
Expand the tracer's proven route table sideways until the daemon serves every surface the phase promises:
the study loop, the day cockpit, and the day cockpit's two mutations — then finish the consolidation by
demoting `itembank serve` and `itembank day` from server owners to daemon launches, so exactly one HTTP
mechanism exists in the codebase.

**User story (MVP framing):** **As a** learner, **I want to** reach my sitting, my flashcards and my day
cockpit from one address, **so that** studying is one tab rather than three processes on three ports.

Purpose: SURF-01 says "one daemon serves every surface on one port". Adding routes gets that literally
true; removing the per-surface servers is what makes it *stay* true. Leaving both mechanisms standing is
the failure mode this plan exists to prevent.
Output: `/study/<stem>`, `/day/<stem>`, `POST /day/<stem>/save`, `POST /day/<stem>/open` on the daemon,
and two CLI commands that reach them instead of duplicating them.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** the assumption-delta scan flagged a singular→plural transition in this phase's scope
("a server per subcommand" → one daemon; "starting the daemon a second time").

**Decision: promote.** The generalization is taken all the way. `surfaces/daemon.py`'s route table
becomes the single HTTP mechanism in the codebase; the per-surface handler classes in
`surfaces/quiz.py` and `surfaces/day.py` are deleted, not kept alongside. `itembank serve BANK` and
`itembank day PLAN` survive as commands — CONTEXT.md's "Claude's Discretion" leaves that open and
SURF-04 wants a CLI entry point for every route — but they become daemon launches scoped to one bank
or one plan rather than owners of their own sockets.

**Rationale:** `add-alongside` is the outcome SURF-01 exists to prevent. `server.py`'s own docstring
already records why the previous duplication mattered: "a port fallback that exists in two places is
one that gets fixed in one of them." Keeping a second handler class alive re-creates that exact debt
one layer up, and every later phase (4, 6, 8, 10) would then have to decide which of two surfaces to
change. `server.py:Handler` and `server.py:bind()` stay untouched and general — they are the shared
primitive, not the second mechanism; D-02 explicitly scopes its change to the daemon's own startup path
and leaves `bind()` intact for other callers such as throwaway servers in tests.
</assumption_delta_decision>
