## Historical Gap Evidence

The original halted summary recorded this blocker verbatim:

> Task 2 says `handle_quiz_answer` returns a server-side page render that can be extended with held-state ladder HTML and a form-post unlock control. The live function returns JSON from `session.do_action`; the quiz question, controls, held state, and ladder are built in JavaScript. There is no server-rendered held card to extend.
>
> Adding the requested behavior would require a new server-side question renderer plus a new HTML transition protocol, or a deliberate progressive-enhancement redesign of the served quiz. Implementing either inside this plan would exceed its declared file and architecture scope and risks violating the one-parser, one-scorer, one-session-adapter boundary.

The revised plan explicitly authorized the progressive-enhancement adapter. The disposition is a presentation-only renderer over `session.do_next` and `session.do_teach`, plus form dispatch through `session.do_action` or `session.do_teach`. No parser, scorer, evidence writer, or entitlement policy was added.
