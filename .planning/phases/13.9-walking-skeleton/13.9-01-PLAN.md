<objective>
Bind one real source from one live fall course to a cited objective map with a
treatment decision per objective, read-only, so plan 02 can author against it
and plan 03 can sit it. This is the first half of the walking skeleton defined
in `READINESS-AUDIT-14A.md` A9 and `ROADMAP.md` Phase 13.9; A9's wording
governs on any divergence.

Decisions already made, not re-litigated here: the skeleton uses shipped
surfaces only, introduces no second parser/scorer/evidence store, and stubs
course-level storage as a human-readable markdown manifest (A9; ROADMAP 13.9
criterion 4). Treatment vocabulary comes from `SOURCE-TO-COURSE.md` "The
course-building loop". The two-file lesson layout default comes from ROADMAP
Phase 3.1 criterion 7.
</objective>
