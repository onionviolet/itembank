## First connected slice

Use a synthetic source-backed unit with one direct reading, an auto-marked item, a wrong-answer permitted hint, one pending short answer and a due review. The learner should move from a wrong response to a relevant cited lesson, answer a changed-context task, leave, reopen the exact sitting and later see the correct review state. Count settled scores, pending work, reading completion and self-rating separately. Browser, packaged, installed, keyboard and human learning-transfer checks remain separate evidence gates.

The next implementation packet should take one F1 inline-completion or matching behavior only after current `fill`, `build` and `dnd` controls are compared against that exact learner task. No donor code, artwork or real question bank is copied by this report. A later code import needs pinned files, license, attribution, dependencies, fallback, update method and removal path. The runtime remains the scorer and disclosure authority.
