# Broader learning-product landscape

Date: 2026-09-08. Status: documentation survey. This lane performed no installations or trials. Subsequent coordinator trials and dispositions supersede the proposed gates below where stated in [ABSORPTION.md](ABSORPTION.md) and [HANDS-ON.md](HANDS-ON.md). This survey supplements the source-level OpenMAIC assessment in [the ecosystem comparison](../OPENMAIC-ECOSYSTEM-COMPARISON-2026-09-08.md).

## Answer

Itembank has serious competition for the visible source-to-learning job. The strongest near-term comparisons are DeepTutor, OpenTutor, Open Notebook, LiaScript, and Docling. They cover different parts of the job, so none is evidence that its assessment, rights, recovery, or accepted-artifact model can replace Itembank. The useful move is a shared-fixture trial, then a bounded reuse decision.

This is a deliberately broad survey, not an exhaustive market claim. Technical statements below come from primary project documentation, repositories, or official product help. Release and README claims are documentation evidence, not a successful trial.

## Method and limits

Discovery queries included English terms such as `open source AI tutor`, `source grounded learning`, `interactive course markdown`, and `PDF to Markdown`, plus Chinese terms `开源 AI 导师`, `开源 课程生成`, `本地 知识库 学习`, `间隔重复 FSRS`, and `PDF 转 Markdown`. Candidate inclusion required a direct fit to at least one product loop: source workspace, course or tutor, interactive authored treatment, assessment or review, publishing, or extraction.

"License verified" means the linked repository or official licensing page was inspected. It does not clear its dependencies, model weights, generated assets, trademarks, source content, or hosted service terms. "Docs only" means exactly that.

## Cross-cutting findings

**F1. Direct alternatives exist now.** DeepTutor, OpenTutor, and Open Notebook make the present learner-facing comparison urgent. Their published capabilities are broad enough that Itembank should measure an end-to-end learner task before building adjacent systems.

**F2. The best reuse boundary is smaller than an app fork.** LiaScript and H5P offer authored interactions. Docling offers source conversion. FSRS offers review scheduling. Each can be trialed behind Itembank's accepted revision, rights, and runtime authority boundaries.

**F3. Licensing changes the shortlist.** DeepTutor, OpenTutor, Open Notebook, LiaScript, Docling, Lumi AI Editor, and Excalidraw are permissive-license leads in this pass. LearnHouse, Moodle, Open edX, eXeLearning, and Anki need a separate integration or distribution decision. MinerU and Marker require extra legal attention.

## Proposed shared-fixture gate

Owner: future comparison coordinator. Status: proposed and unexecuted.

1. Use one public-domain or synthetic, citation-ready source with two explicit objectives.
2. Ask each direct product candidate to produce a reading, grounded explanation, one interaction, and a short practice activity under comparable model settings where possible.
3. Record setup time, source locator accuracy, manual corrections, objective coverage, model or hosting cost, and learner-visible recovery after a failed generation.
4. Test reload/resume, offline or exported artifact behavior, accessibility of the interaction, and whether a product reveals or settles assessment content outside Itembank's runtime.
5. Preserve each result as a versioned evidence packet. Promote a component only if its stable interface passes the gate. Consider a sidecar or fork only if its whole learner flow wins while authority, rights, recovery, and maintenance costs remain lower.

No source code, product configuration, learner data, or external account was changed. This report is reversible by deleting this file. No candidate has been accepted, rejected, installed, or benchmarked.
