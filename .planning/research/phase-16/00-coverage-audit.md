## Cross-cutting additions found by audit

These were not stated as separate requests, but could invalidate the proposed
product if left implicit. Stream 11 owns their research:

- Learner control and personalization boundaries.
- Motivation without punitive or manipulative mechanics.
- Privacy, source rights, media licensing, and generated provenance.
- Search and recommendation quality at large collection sizes.
- Localization and culturally or linguistically varied material.
- Multi-device, synchronization, sharing, and collaboration boundaries.
- Performance, reliability, storage growth, backups, and recovery.
- Learning-effectiveness and authoring-quality evaluation without telemetry.
- Long-term maintenance and dependency cost.

## Audit questions for synthesis

1. Did every explicit idea receive evidence, a disposition, and an owner?
2. Did any stream assume another stream would answer a question that neither
   actually answered?
3. Are facts, user preferences, research conclusions, and implementation
   decisions visibly distinct?
4. Are rejected and deferred ideas preserved with reasons and revisit triggers?
5. Does every accepted capability have a portable form, accessible behavior,
   authoring rule, validation method, and upgrade path?
