<objective>
Fix four defects found by driving a live `itembank daemon` against the fixtures
directory, and add the stdlib regression coverage that would have caught the two
silent ones without a browser.

The four defects (ids D1-D4 come from the task brief and are used verbatim
throughout this plan):

- **D1** — a stray `}` in `surfaces/lesson.py` closes `@media print{` one rule
  early, leaking seven print rules into the screen stylesheet. The leaked
  `[popover]{display:none}` is an author rule and outranks the UA
  `[popover]:not(:popover-open){display:none}`, so the glossary panel never
  renders even when open. The reader section nav and the back-to-text links are
  also invisible on screen and callout shadows are flattened.
- **D2** — `model.parse_terms` scans preamble rows to the end of the preamble
  with no stop at the next `##` heading, so a `## TERMS` section placed above
  `## LESSON` swallows every pipe row in the lesson body as a glossary entry.
- **D3** — the four vendored `@font-face` urls are relative, so on a nested
  route such as `/lesson/<stem>` they resolve to `/lesson/fonts/...`; the daemon
  has no font route at all. Four 404s per page load; the reader has never
  rendered in its intended typefaces under the daemon.
- **D4** — with no model backend reachable, the quiz help panel renders
  `Authored hint` followed by `the-airway-step-by-step`: `runtime.authored_hint`
  tier 0 returns the derived lesson **slug** as its content, and
  `quiz_page.authoredHtml` prints that content verbatim.

Two further findings, confirmed while reading the code and folded into scope
because they are the same defect as D2 in a sibling function:

- `model.parse_sources` (the `## SOURCES` registry) and `model.parse_cases`
  (the `## CASES` registry) carry the identical unbounded row scan. The fix is
  one shared bounded-section helper with three callers, not three patches.
- `quiz_page.authoredHtml` returns the empty string whenever
  `authored.content` is not a string. Tier 5's content is the
  `explain_payload` dict, so after the authored reveal the panel silently
  renders nothing. That is the "correct empty state" half of D4.

Purpose: the reader has been shipping with its glossary suppressed, its fonts
404ing and its offline hint printing an internal identifier. All three are
invisible to the current suite because nothing asserts over emitted CSS.

Output: four fixes, one new stdlib test file, one new fixture, and assertions
added to four existing roundtrips.
</objective>
