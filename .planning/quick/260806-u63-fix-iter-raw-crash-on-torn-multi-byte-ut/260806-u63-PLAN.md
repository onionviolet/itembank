<objective>
Close the one confirmed blocker from Phase 1 verification: `evidence.iter_raw()` — the read
primitive every evidence consumer in this codebase is built on — raises an uncaught
`UnicodeDecodeError` when the log's final line was truncated mid multi-byte UTF-8 character,
instead of skipping and reporting that line. This directly falsifies the module's own documented
D-09 guarantee ("a malformed line is skipped and reported, never fatal") and the "auditable" half
of EVID-05: a crash does not lose bytes on disk, but it can make the entire evidence store
unreadable through every surface until manually repaired.

The defect is already diagnosed and reproduced three times independently (code reviewer,
orchestrator, verifier). Do not re-investigate whether it is real.

Purpose: `iter_raw` is the single chokepoint — one keyword restores the guarantee for every reader
at once. Equally important is the test lesson: the existing kill probe pads lines with pure ASCII,
and ASCII cannot be torn mid-character, which is precisely why a passing suite gated a broken
guarantee.

Output: a one-keyword fix in `evidence.py`, a deterministic regression probe that goes RED before
the fix and GREEN after, and a kill probe that carries genuine multi-byte content.

`with open(path, encoding="utf-8") as fh:` with no `errors=` argument. The decode happens during
