# User vision inbox

**Purpose:** preserve rough ideaboarding that may affect product intent before
deciding whether it belongs in the authoritative vision record.

**Rule:** entries stay verbatim. Review them after an ideaboarding pass. Promote
statements about outcomes, experience, scope, values, boundaries, users,
success, or unresolved product direction. Route implementation ideas, research
leads, and task mechanics to their owning documents. A promoted entry gets a
dated disposition and link; it is not deleted from this inbox.

## Disposition vocabulary for this inbox

- **Promote:** add the verbatim statement and interpretation to the vision record.
- **Route:** preserve here and link to its research, decision, requirement, or
  operational owner.
- **Split:** promote the product-intent clauses and route the implementation or
  task clauses.
- **Hold:** keep unresolved until more ideaboarding or research changes its
  meaning.
- **Duplicate:** link to the earlier vision statement it restates without adding
  another authoritative copy.

Note: this inbox vocabulary is about where a raw statement goes. It is distinct
from the seven-way idea disposition ledger (Core, Registered, Prototype,
Backburner, Deferred, Superseded, Rejected), which routes analyzed proposals,
not raw quotations.
