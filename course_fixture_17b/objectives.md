# Unit 3 objectives: Peat Hydrology and the Lantern Moss Cycle

Seven unit objectives, all `required` under membership version AFE101-m1
(see `scope.md`). Each carries a citation into one of the two synthetic
sources; anchors are the heading slugs of the cited sections.

1. **O3.1** Explain why the Aldrasse fen is minerotrophic and how lateral
   mineral inflow, rather than rainfall, sets its porewater chemistry and
   plant community.
   [SRC: sources/fen_hydrology_field_notes.md#basin-overview]

2. **O3.2** Describe the two-layer acrotelm and catotelm structure and
   use it to predict how the water table responds to a storm event versus
   seasonal recharge.
   [SRC: sources/fen_hydrology_field_notes.md#water-table-dynamics]

3. **O3.3** State the drought threshold for the fen (a water table more
   than twenty five centimeters below the surface) and identify how the
   vegetation registers an excursion past it.
   [SRC: sources/fen_hydrology_field_notes.md#water-table-dynamics]

4. **O3.4** Explain why peat accumulation tracks water table position
   rather than surface productivity, and estimate profile age from an
   accumulation rate.
   [SRC: sources/fen_hydrology_field_notes.md#peat-accumulation-and-decay]

5. **O3.5** Describe the lagg zone's two roles, chemical gatekeeper and
   early-warning line, and state the conductance condition at station A1
   that triggers a full transect resample.
   [SRC: sources/fen_hydrology_field_notes.md#mineral-inflow-and-the-lagg-boundary]

6. **O3.6** Sequence the four phases of the lantern moss annual cycle
   and explain why the early-summer water position is the hinge on which
   a cushion's reproductive wager turns.
   [SRC: sources/lantern_moss_survey.md#annual-cycle]

7. **O3.7** Use lantern moss vitality scores as a hydrological
   instrument: state the station-mean threshold treated as a drought
   equivalent and connect it to the dipwell threshold in the hydrology
   notes.
   [SRC: sources/lantern_moss_survey.md#hydrological-sensitivity]
