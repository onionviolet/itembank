The idea

Time is shared. Speed is not.

Why distance grows with time

Two walkers start together. Walker A travels at 3 metres per second; Walker B travels at 5 metres per second. Both keep a steady speed.

Distance is speed multiplied by elapsed time. Give each walker the same amount of time and the faster walker covers more ground.

distance = speed × timemetres = metres/second × seconds
