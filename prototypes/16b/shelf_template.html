Contract check

checking Scanning the shelf for a percent character.

16B-04 asserts that no shelf card, chip, or resume cue contains a percent character, and that no single aggregate completion, mastery, or readiness percentage appears on a card. That prohibition carries status: kept. This page runs the same check against its own DOM.

What could not be sourced, and why that matters

Three fields on every card are illustration

  • Attention state. course_shelf_state belongs to plan 16B-04 and does not exist. The 16B-01 precondition check halts on Phase 16A, so nothing downstream of it has run.
  • Resume cue. Needs evidence, and course.py imports no evidence by design, so the course sidecar structurally cannot answer it. Whatever computes the cue will be a third thing reading both.
  • Last activity date. Nothing in graph.py holds a date. This is the open half of IL-20260826-07, and it is also why due has no source.

This is the mockup's most useful output. The structural half of the shelf is fully backed by shipped code today; the entire attention-and-resume half, which is what makes a shelf worth looking at, has no data behind it yet.
