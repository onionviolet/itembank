02 / MAKE SENSE OF IT

A comparison you can explain.

AI-authored synthesis from RAIN-01. Open either step when you want the reasoning. These controls do not submit an answer.

Why hold time equal?

RAIN-01 asks for equal intervals. That lets this comparison focus on depth over the same duration. A larger depth over a longer interval would mix two changing quantities.

How does the difference work?

Subtract A from B. At the source values, 18 − 12 = 6 mm. B is higher by 6 mm. At B = 12 mm the difference is zero. At B = 6 mm, B is lower by 6 mm. The last two values are hypothetical examples, not extra source observations.
