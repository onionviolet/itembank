Reading: the airway, step by step

Stridor
A high, harsh sound on that means the upper airway is narrowed. Check whether the airway is still before you do anything else.

Watch for this. A quiet chest in a patient who was noisy a minute ago is a worsening sign, not an improving one. Treat it as an change.

Before reading on: which sound on would worry you most in a patient who cannot speak?

Check yourself

Which finding most clearly suggests the airway is at risk?

  • Clear speech in full sentences
  • Stridor with weak effort
  • Regular chest rise
