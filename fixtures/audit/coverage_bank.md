Q2. What is the first maneuver to open an airway in a trauma patient?
[ID: 853253e1c41b48f2]
[HASH: sha256:d5a00bf8f65b0755]
[OBJECTIVE: emt:airway.opa]
A) Jaw-thrust
B) Head-tilt chin-lift
C) Nasopharyngeal airway
D) Oropharyngeal airway
CORRECT: A
WHY BEST: Jaw-thrust opens the airway without moving a possibly injured cervical spine.
KEY DISCRIMINATOR: The item turns on cervical-spine precautions in trauma.
SECOND-BEST: Head-tilt chin-lift would be correct in a medical (non-trauma) arrest.
DISTRACTOR ANALYSIS:
- B) Head-tilt chin-lift would be correct if no spinal injury were suspected.
- C) A nasopharyngeal airway would be correct after the airway is opened.
- D) An oropharyngeal airway would be correct in an unconscious patient without a gag reflex.
TRAP: Learners choose the fastest maneuver, not the safest for trauma.
CONFIDENCE: high
