# EMT Unit 1: Airway Management

- emt:airway.opa: The learner opens and maintains a patent airway
- emt:airway.adjunct: The learner selects the correct airway adjunct
- emt:airway.ventilation: The learner provides adequate ventilation

The student should be able to prioritize airway management during a cardiac arrest because an unsecured airway causes hypoxic injury.
